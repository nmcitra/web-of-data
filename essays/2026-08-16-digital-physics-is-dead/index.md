Digital Physics Is Dead
On what survived an audit of my own framework
There is a file with your name on it, and it is not you.
It knows your address, or an address you used to have. It knows a number that stands for your income, which was true on a Tuesday in March. It knows whether you have a child, and it does not know that the child moved out, or moved back, or that the reason for either was something no field on the form asks about.
In Minnesota a nonprofit and the sites it sponsored billed a federal child-nutrition program for a quarter of a billion dollars.[¹] One restaurant was reimbursed for as many as twenty thousand meals a day. When the FBI put that building under surveillance, they counted about forty people a day going in and out, and they counted for six weeks. By the time anyone measured the distance between the filings and the buildings, the money meant for feeding children had gone into houses and cars and jewelry and property overseas.
Somebody stood in that gap for two years, and the gap was the whole business.
Now run it the other way. In Michigan an automated system called MiDAS decided that forty thousand people had committed unemployment fraud. It was wrong about ninety-three percent of them.[²] The state garnished wages, seized tax refunds, and charged penalties of four times what it said people owed. The settlement was approved in January of 2024 - twenty million dollars, shared among about three thousand of them.
The people MiDAS falsely accused had not defrauded the state. 
The state simply believed its own file over them, and the file won, and it kept winning for nine years.
Those two are the same object seen from opposite directions. 
One is a record that outran the world and got monetized. Stolen money.
The other is a record that outran the world and got enforced. Stolen time, standing, and a loss of trust.

In both cases the damage lived in the distance between a person and their paperwork, and in both cases nobody was measuring that distance until it was much too late.
I have a file too. Mine is public.
It said: Trust as a governable, physics-based primitive. It says: Digital Physics is not a metaphor - it is an engineering discipline. I wrote those sentences. I meant them. Some linger on my website while I write this.
The file is updated on a schedule. I am updated continuously.
So, the two of us drift.
My file costs me an argument. Michigan's system falsely accused forty thousand people and cost many of them wages, tax refunds, and years. I am not claiming those are the same size; I am claiming they are the same shape and have the same mechanism. Mine is the only one I have standing to open.
Fraud lives in that distance. 
Lying is a thing you do with your mouth; fraud at scale is often a thing you do with the space between a body and its paperwork. You find the place where the two have come apart, you stand in it, and you operate there for as long as nobody thinks to measure the distance of the gap.
I've been working this gap for my entire career; professionally inside the gap: between the human and the machine. Even before that, when I was twelve and happened to type the word "cat" into an Apple IIe prompt, until now. 
One thing I've noticed is that nothing in the gap stays still. It doesn't hold, or pause, or freeze. Everything is continuously moving. The record moves. The person moves. They move at different rates, in different directions, for different reasons, and the whole problem turns out to be a problem of things in motion relative to each other - every honest sentence about it has a rate in it somewhere, a direction, a distance opening or closing between two objects that are both going somewhere without checking with each other first.
In November of 2025 I wrote a line I did not understand the weight of at the time: identity isn't a snapshot, it's a trajectory. That was before any of the vocabulary in this essay existed. I was reaching beyond a metaphor.
There is a language for that. It is the oldest one we have for things in motion.
So I reached for it.

---

I called the framework Digital Physics.
Not as a decoration. I meant it, and I wrote it down in the places where meaning it costs something. The public record is accurate about the man who made it. What changed was me.
I want that stated before anything else. There is a genre of essay where a person softens what they used to think until it sounds like what they think now, and the softening happens so gradually that no single sentence is a lie.
This is not going to be that.

---

The audit
In August '26, I ran an attack on my own vocabulary. A seek and destroy kind of approach.
The method was simple and the results were not comfortable. Take one of your own terms. Set it beside the strongest established analogue already doing the same claimed work. Ask which one a stranger would reach for if both were lying on the table in front of them.
Fourteen constructs from the framework went in. Two AI reviewers worked without seeing each other's tables - one a blind commercial model under explicit kill instructions, one my own assistant. Both tables were committed before either result was shared. If a term carried its own weight, it would survive contact with its nearest analogue. If it was a new coat on an old idea, it would not.
Eight of the fourteen died in both passes.
E_base, my composite trust score, mapped onto established credit-risk composition: weighted history, backing, thin-file caps. Deautomation mapped onto adaptive automation and fault-driven degradation. Sponsorship mapped onto surety, with a stake that could be lost. The Kinetic Envelope combined familiar security moves: no_new_privs and capability attenuation. The Context Tensor was multi-criteria decision analysis wearing a mathematical name it had not earned. Nothing about the object transformed like a tensor, which is the kind of thing a person can check in an afternoon and which I had not.
I published all of that. The kills are on the site, in detail, with the comparison tables. I thought that was honest, for a man asking other people to take his framework seriously.
You might be asking why I hadn't done this before. It's a great question. And the answer is: I did. Here's what changed: consumer products are better now than three months ago, six months ago. And two, what my assistant knows now vs. January '26. I suppose the third reason is what I've learned doing the work and being able to ask sharper questions.
Eight died. I published all eight.

---

So let me ask you the question I could not answer, and let me ask it in the middle, where it is inconvenient.
You know the shape of what I was looking at: two objects in motion relative to each other, a gap between them, a rate at which they separate or come closer, a threshold past which the separation matters. A boundary between your digital self, other digital selves, and the digital environment itself.
Does that sound like physics to you?
I am not asking rhetorically. I have been at this since the fall of 2025, and I do not know. Here is why I do not know, and it is worse than merely not having enough evidence yet.
Suppose I get the evidence. 
Suppose the thing I am building predicts a number in advance, for a system it was never tuned on, and the number comes back right. Someone will say: that is a good model. Models are not physics. Epidemiology predicts thresholds, and nobody calls an outbreak a force. They will not be obviously wrong.
Now suppose it fails. Someone will say: the model was bad; the physics was never the issue. They will not be obviously wrong either.
That is the part that should bother you. A word whose presence and absence are both consistent with every available outcome is not doing any measuring, whatever else it may be doing.
There is no experiment that returns the word. There are real criteria, however: 
operational quantities, 
falsifiable predictions, 
replication by somebody who is not me, and
results that survive a legitimate change of description. 

A model meeting those criteria is a better model. What no single experiment settles is the moment a good model has earned the stronger name.
I have looked for that line. I cannot find it. I have not found anybody who uses the word freely and can point to the moment either.
My own framework has a name for this kind of question. An aporia - a productive impasse, a road with no through. The deepest questions are not locks but horizons. A lock has a key. A horizon changes the walker instead of closing the distance.
I am telling you I am walking toward a horizon.
I am not telling you I arrived.
And I want to be plain about the cost, because it is not nothing. That sentence - this is physics, not policy - was the most useful one I owned. It was the one that made engineers sit forward in meetings. I have traded it for a question I cannot answer and a bar I have not cleared.

---

What survived
The word was doing two jobs, and only one of them was earned.
As a claim - an assertion that this is what the world is made of - it had no support, and I have withdrawn it.
As an image, it made a smaller promise. A metaphor does not have to prove what the world is made of. It still has to earn the work it does.

That distinction is the whole of what changed, and it is why this is not an obituary.
The audit did not take the terms away from me. Sponsorship is still called sponsorship; I just have to write surety beside it now. The Kinetic Envelope still does the job it did before, under a sentence that says security confinement plus attenuation out loud. What died in those eight cases was the claim of novelty, not the object and not necessarily the name. The words kept working the moment they stopped pretending to be new.
That is the same operation, one level down, that I have now run on the masthead.
Kinetic Trust Protocol. Kinetic Envelope. Trust in motion. Governance in motion. Worldline. Trajectory. The hull.
The audit stripped away the novelty claims and left a vocabulary organized around motion and boundaries. That does not prove those objects are new. It tells me what the framework had been trying to describe before I knew how to say it without overclaiming.

---

There is a field, and there is your line through it. 
The digital world is not your path through the digital world. 
The feed is an environment; your trajectory is your motion inside it. 
If you cannot tell those apart, your worldline is not yours. It is the shape the environment pressed into you while you were reporting that you had decided something.
Whoever turns the model turns the mind leaning on it, and both swear nothing moved.
That is the part that should worry anyone building these systems, because it does not require a villain, a conspiracy, or even a bad quarter - it only requires an environment that adjusts faster than the person inside it can notice the adjustment, which is a fair description of every feed, every recommender, and every agent now being handed a corporate credential.
Which is an admission I owe. The core law of my own framework says autonomy must not exceed environment: A ≤ E.
I still think it may be a law. But a bound only becomes operational when both sides can be defined and measured apart. Until then, A ≤ E returns a quotation instead of a decision.
I do not think I was hiding that. I think I had been standing on it long enough that it looked like the floor.

---

And the gap is not the flaw. That one took me a while.
My wife Nayomi takes pottery classes; seasons of Wednesdays. She comes home with plates and bowls, and I have spent more time than is reasonable thinking about what makes one useful to people. 
It is not the clay. It is the empty space.

---

The seam between you and your record is a line. You cannot see it today - not with anything most people are handed. The reason I began building a protocol was that a line nobody can see is a line anybody can move. The aim is to give that seam a cryptographic edge - to make it something a person can point to and contest. The instrument and machinery that would do that for ordinary people does not exist yet.
That is what the tether is meant to provide. It is a thing that has to be witnessed, in a place, over time, at cost - and Proof of Presence is not a claim you make about yourself.
You can distinguish yourself from your digital self only because there is room between you.
Every instinct in my industry is to close that gap. Shut it down. Bind the identity harder. Tighten the fidelity. Make the record and the person the same object.
Here is what that instinct misses. The observer has a worldline too. A system watching you is not a view from nowhere - it is another trajectory, moving, accumulating, leaving its own trail, and it can be asked the same questions it asks you. Nothing about the asymmetry is physical. We design these systems. We build them. A trajectory can be sovereign because somebody decided it would be, and the ones that are not are that way for the same reason.

---

So fraud lives in the gap, and you cannot fix fraud by removing the gap, because removing the gap removes the person.
That is the sentence six years bought. Here is what I think it asks of you.
Take possession of your own worldline. Know what the records say. Know where they came from and how old they are. Ask what is being decided about you and on what evidence, and keep asking, because the distance between your body and your digital worldline is not going to measure itself and nobody has been assigned to do that on your behalf. 
It is the one part of this that nobody can walk for you.

---

Recognition is a frame of reference.
Without a frame, motion is not zero. It is undefined. Is the train moving or is the platform? The question is not hard. It is meaningless until you say what you are measuring against.
Recognition is the operation of granting somebody's account standing - the right to be heard, before and separate from the question of whether they are right. It is not belief. It admits the account into the record; it does not certify it.
Recognition decides whether the subject's own account is admitted as a reference frame.
Withhold recognition and keep watching, and the watching does not stop.
That is the trap. The institution still has a frame - its own - and inside that frame you can be measured continuously and with great confidence and precision. 
What is missing is the other frame. Your account of your own motion is not evidence. There is no admissible position from which you can say: that is not what happened, and here is what did.
Watched continuously, admitted never. Measured without standing to contest the measurement.
And notice how ordinary that arrangement is - a credit file, a risk score, a content moderation appeal that routes to the same model that made the call in the first place, a benefits determination you can dispute only by supplying more of the evidence that was already used against you.
That is not merely a moral complaint about surveillance. It is a structural result: one frame, one direction, and the party being described has no admissible account of their own trajectory.

---

And here is how you cross a gap you cannot see across.
The gap makes intent unreachable. You are on this side of it, and whatever anybody meant is on the other. Every institution that has tried to police the seam eventually reaches the same wall and gets told to prove a state of mind.
So stop.
There is an animal in my notes that evades the hunter by ceasing to be a single body anyone can point at - each hoof a separate LLC, the rack leased, the heartbeat a managed service. You cannot field-dress a nexus of contracts.
But the elk still has to eat.
The eating leaves a trail, and every strand of that trail points toward the dark patch where the advantage pooled.
Do not read the mind. Read the integral.
That inversion is the piece I would defend hardest, because it currently makes a claim somebody else can check. Run it on a benefits case and it names who was fed without anyone testifying to what they intended. Run it on a Pueblo's name sitting on a Delaware shell and, in the Acoma case, the represented community objected and the filing was renamed. Run it on your own model of a person you love and it says the same thing it says everywhere else: go and check the representation against the thing it represents, by contact, not by inference.
It breaks in two places, and I would rather name them than have you find them.
If somebody invents a person who never existed, there is no seam - nothing on one side to differ from the other - and checking the record against the human fails, because there is no human. That is a different crime in similar clothes.
And the method needs the advantage to land somewhere. You find fraud by finding where it pooled. If a gain disperses and never collects, there is no pool to find. That is why I think this transfers to AI agents at all: they have to eat. Compute, tokens, money. Something gets spent, and spending leaves a mark. I would rather rest the claim on what a system has to consume than on anything anyone believes about what it is.

---

So what would settle any of this?
There is one bar I know I would accept: predict a threshold in advance, in a system the model was never fitted to, with the number fixed before anyone sees the target data, then have the result replicated by somebody with no stake in my being right.
Other tests would count. I am naming the one I cannot argue my way around.
That is the discipline. A framework that predicts after reading is a framework that confirms itself.
I have a paper out that formalizes part of the work, and it is missing the piece everyone asks about because the full instrumented form is still being built. It will be published. Not held back - unfinished, which is a different thing, and the only honest reason not to show someone your work.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21209147Clearing that bar will not make the word true. I said this in the middle and I meant it: nobody knows how much evidence turns a good description into a physics. What clearing the bar would do is make the question worth arguing about with people who currently have no reason to.
That is all I want.

---

I should say what has been built, because the honest version of "I have not cleared the bar" is that I am not the only one carrying it anymore.
The specifications are public and version two shipped on the fourteenth of August. Mike Storm, a KTP collaborator, is building on it in stealth. Robin Martherus at Tamed Autonomy is building the layer above mine - I did the CAN, he is doing the SHOULD. SINT is using the environmental veto for robot and tool authorization. There is work going into OWASP's quantum AI initiative, and into Buzz, Block's open-source runtime.
None of that makes it physics.
So let me draw the outline instead. Here is the shape of what is missing, traced around the empty place where the thing would be.
Autonomy measured apart from environment, so the bound returns a decision instead of a quotation. 
A number predicted before anyone has seen the data it is meant to predict. 
The instrumented form, finished and published, so you can check the arithmetic rather than take my word for the shape of it. 
An instrument that lets a person see the line between themselves and their record without having to be the one who built the instrument. 
And somebody who is not me, and does not want me to be right, finding the same shape from a starting point I never stood on.

That is five absences in the shape of a physical body. Everything I have is the chalk.

---

I retired a claim I had not earned. I kept an image, and a vocabulary that turned out to be organized around motion and boundaries the whole time.
And I am still thinking about the children in Minnesota who were a quarter of a billion dollars short - fed on paper, twenty thousand meals a day, in a building the FBI watched forty people a day walk into.
Nobody was measuring the distance. That is the whole game. 
The algorithm, the criminals, and the state were different actors. The shared failure was a distance everybody could have measured but nobody did until it was too late.
That distance is still there. It is between your body and your digital worldline. It keeps moving and expanding while you read this, and there is no instrument in your hands right now that would tell you by how much.
There is another crossing I have not written yet. A real gorge. A real bridge. The same measurement. Next essay.
I would take a better answer than mine. I would take it gladly, from anyone.
Long live Digital Physics. 
Show me your physics.
